Sample Unit: Fractions and Decimals

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to convert between fractions and decimals and solve word problems involving these concepts.

Warm-Up

Discuss where we see fractions and decimals in real life (prices, measurements, probabilities).

Convert the following fractions to decimals:

1/2

3/4

7/8

Convert the following decimals to fractions (simplify where possible):

0.2

0.75

0.125

Challenge

Solve the word problem below and show your work.

At a bake sale, 3/5 of the cookies were sold in the morning and 0.2 of the remaining were sold in the afternoon. What fraction of the total cookies were sold in the afternoon?
